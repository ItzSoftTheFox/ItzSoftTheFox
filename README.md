## <img src="assets/about.svg" width="22" height="22" alt="" /> About me

I'm **Michael Krahula**, known online as **ItzSoftTheFox** — an IT student who enjoys turning unfamiliar problems into things that work.

Currently a 4th-year IT student at **SOŠ EDUCAnet Brno**, focused on **web development**, studying since **September 2023**.

I'm drawn to backend logic, desktop applications and the systems behind them. I enjoy building interfaces too, but understanding how everything fits together is what keeps me interested. My next direction is bringing software closer to hardware: exploring PC components, embedded systems and the connection between code and physical devices.

**Curiosity drives me. Patience keeps me going.** I learn by building, asking questions and staying with a problem until I understand it.
